# Ihsan MUN 2026 Blueprint

## Product Focus

- Present Ihsan Schools Model United Nations as a premium, editorial-style conference site
- Keep registration paths clear for delegates, chairs, staff, and organizing team members
- Showcase committees, secretariat members, venue information, and core conference resources
- Support light editorial updates through authenticated image replacement

## Experience Principles

- Use a cinematic landing experience with strong typography and restrained motion
- Keep navigation simple, fast, and readable on both desktop and mobile
- Make every page feel consistent through shared surface styles, spacing, and color hierarchy
- Favor clear calls to action over decorative UI noise

## Visual Direction

- Base palette: deep burgundy and wine-toned neutrals
- Accent palette: warm champagne highlights for key emphasis and labels
- Typography: Cinzel for display headings, Inter for body copy and interface text
- Surfaces: layered panels, soft blur, subtle grid overlays, and restrained glow

## Content Areas

- Home: Hero, countdown, conference introduction, committee highlights, registration CTA
- About: Mission, values, institutional context, visual storytelling
- Committees: Committee catalog, agenda topics, preparation resources
- Registration: Role selection with pricing overview and external form routing
- Secretariat: Leadership and team presentation
- Venue and Contact: Practical information with direct outreach paths
